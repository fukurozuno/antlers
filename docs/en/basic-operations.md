# Basic operations

## Panes and cursor

Use `Tab` to switch the active pane, or `Left` and `Right` to activate a specific pane. Move the cursor with `Up`, `Down`, `PageUp`, and `PageDown`.

By default, `Enter` opens the selected directory. Use `Backspace` to go to the parent directory. `Command+Left` and `Command+Right` move backward and forward through navigation history.

## Preview files

Press `V` to open an in-pane preview for the selected file. Press `Esc` or `Q` to return to the file list.

Press `Shift+V` to show or hide the auxiliary preview pane. It follows the selection in the active pane. For files, it shows a Quick Look preview plus the name, kind, size, modification date, and path. For a directory or no selection, it shows item counts and the current path.

Choose the preview pane position in General settings. Press `Option+V` to enter preview mode, then use `Option+Left` and `Option+Right` to adjust the divider between the preview and file lists.

## Selection and marking

Use `Space` to mark the item at the cursor. By default, the cursor then advances to the next item. `Shift+Space` marks an item and moves to the previous item. Mark several items before copying, moving, or sending them to the Trash.

## Navigate quickly

- `J`: show bookmarks
- In the bookmark list, use `1` through `9` and `0` to choose the first ten entries
- `Shift+J`: enter a path directly
- `H`: show the active pane's history
- `O`: set the active pane to the opposite pane's path

Press `L` to open Locations. When a removable volume is selected, press `Command+E` and confirm to eject it.

Press `T` to open Finder tags and search by the selected tag. `Shift+T` adds or removes a tag from the selected item.

## Search, filter, and mark

Press `F` to start incremental search. Choose prefix, contains, or exact matching in General settings.

`Shift+@` starts wildcard marking and `Shift+:` starts a file mask. Both support `*` and `?`, and confirmed patterns are saved in a shared history. Click the input field, or press `Up` or `Down`, to show the history; press `Return` to reuse a pattern.
